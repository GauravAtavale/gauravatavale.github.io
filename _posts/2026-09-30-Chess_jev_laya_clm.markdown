Two weeks ago, on September 15, TypeSafe AI came out of stealth with $40M in seed funding led by DCVC and a model called Jev, and it felt like the industry changed overnight. Jev isn't a language model in the usual sense. It doesn't generate text; it returns calibrated probabilities over options you define in advance, an approach TypeSafe calls a "System One" model. On its own workflow evaluations, TypeSafe claims Jev is 193.6 times faster and 444.6 times cheaper than using a large language model, and it charges $0.042 per million input tokens. The reaction was immediate: two days after launch, its launch video had 36 million views, and nine days in, The Information reported that TypeSafe was negotiating a round of $1 billion or more at a valuation of around $10 billion.
